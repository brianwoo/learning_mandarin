        下午好。今天我们首先讲解了之前作业中容易出错的地方。同学们在做练习时，需要更加专注，避免出现将“买了”误写成“买子”的情况；也要多观察汉字结构，比如书写“包”，需要最后一画（竖弯钩）包裹住上面的汉字结构，而不是第二画的横折钩包裹住下面。随着我们学习的汉字越来越多，也需要孩子们在复习时多留意易字形相似的汉字，如“真”和“具”，“子”和“了”，“间”和“问”等。也需要区别同音不同义的汉字，如“是”、“事”和“室”等。

       我们今天重点学习了第六课的11个生字（会、事、可、别、收、拾、房、间、还、洗、饿）的读音、含义和书写。学习了三个新的偏旁部首：“扌”(表示手的动作或与手有关的事物)；“户”(与门、家、房屋或出入有关)；“饣”(与饮食或者与食物相关)。认识部首有助于帮助孩子猜测和理解汉字的含义。

Hi Parents and Students,  

Good afternoon! Today, we reviewed common mistakes in the previous homework. Students need to focus more during practice to avoid errors such as writing 买了 (bought) as 买子. Students should also pay attention to character structures. For example, when writing 包 (bag), the final stroke (vertical hook: 竖弯钩) should wrap around the upper structure, not the second stroke (horizontal hook: 横折钩) wrapping the bottom part.  

As we learn more characters, it’s essential to notice similar-looking ones during review. Examples include: 真 and 具, 子 and 了, 间 and 问 etc. Students also need to differentiate homophones with different meanings, such as 是 (is), 事 (matter), and 室 (room).  

We also focused on Lesson 6, learning 11 new characters (会、事、可、别、收、拾、房、间、还、洗、饿)—their pronunciation, meanings, and writing. We also introduced three new radicals:  
- 扌: related to hand actions or things done by hand.  
- 户: related to doors, houses, or entry/exit.  
- 饣: related to food or eating.  

Recognizing radicals helps students guess and understand character meanings.  

Through reading the text 我会做的事 (Things I Can Do), we practiced the sentence pattern:  
“Subject + 会 + Verb + Object” . For example, “我会收拾房间” (I can tidy the room). Here, 会 is a modal verb that indicates knowing how to do or being able to do something. We practiced replacing words in the sentence to create new ones.
