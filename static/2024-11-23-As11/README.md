Today we learned 14 characters from Lesson 5: 买 (buy), 岁 (age), 昨 (yesterday), 给 (give), 蛋 (egg), 包 (bag), 果 (fruit), 玩 (play), 具 (tool), 哥 (older brother), 姐 (older sister), 张 (sheet), 卡 (card), and 唱 (sing). We practiced their pronunciation, meanings, and writing, as well as tried forming words with them.  

During writing, we focused on:  
1.The third stroke of 纟 (silk radical), 女 (female radical), and the fourth stroke of 王 (king radical) being upward stroke (提), not horizontal line (横).  
2.The character 具 has three horizontal strokes inside, while the right part of 姐 has two horizontal strokes.  
3.The radical of 包 is 勹 (wrap frame) and it is written from the outside first, then the inside.
