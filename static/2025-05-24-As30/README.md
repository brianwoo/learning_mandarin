We also worked in groups to practice a dialogue based on the topic of travel, using the words and sentence patterns on page 93 of the textbook.

 

At the end of class, we practiced a short play called "我绝不开门" (We Will Never Open the Door). During this activity, students learned six new characters: 绝 (never), 狼 (wolf), 乖 (well-behaved), 互 (mutual), 相 (each other), 眼 (eye). We also discussed what might happen after the sentence: “狼失望地走开了。” (The wolf walked away in disappointment.)
