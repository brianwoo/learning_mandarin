下午好。今天我们首先讲解了上周家庭作业中容易出现的错误。之后我们进入了中文第八单元的学习。

本课我们学习了课文《月亮会不会跟人走》，孩子们掌握了13个生字（亮、跟、也、为、什、么、很、远、哪、都、觉、地、明）的读音和含义。随后，我们一起练习了这些汉字的书写，并进行了组词练习。在练习本课生字时，还需要孩子们区分以下同音或近音字：“亮”和“两”； “远”、“园”和“元”；“哪”和“那”。此外，我们还学习了汉字偏旁部首“足”字旁和“土”字旁。“足”字旁通常与行走或腿脚动作有关，书写时最后两笔的撇和捺需要改写为竖和提；而“土”字旁通常与土地、建筑、自然环境等含义相关，书写时最后一笔改横为提。

在课文中，孩子们需要注意多音字“地”的用法：当“地”读轻声de时，用在副词后面（adverb + 地 + verb），如“高兴地说”；请区别第七课中的“得de”，用在动词后面（verb + 得 + adverb），如“做得好”。另外，“得”也可以组成动词词组“觉得”，表示认为或产生某种感觉。

 

另外，我们还学习了句型“月亮会不会跟人走？”这是一个正反疑问句，用谓语的肯定与否定形式并列（会不会）来表示疑问。要求孩子们能够参考例句造句，并做出肯定（“会。”）或否定（“不会。”）的回答。下节课会继续练习这个句型的用法。

Hi Parents and Students,



Good afternoon! Today, we first went over common mistakes from last week’s homework to help students understand and correct them. Then, we moved on to Lesson 8 in our Chinese textbook.



In this lesson, we studied the text “Does the Moon Follow Us?” and learned 13 new Chinese characters: 亮、跟、也、为、什、么、很、远、哪、都、觉、地、明. The students practiced their pronunciation and meanings. After that, we worked on writing these characters and completed word formation exercises.



While practicing the new characters, students need to pay special attention to similar-sounding words:”亮”and”两”; “远”,”园”and”元”；”哪”and”那”.



We also introduced two important radicals: “足” (zú) radical, which is related to walking or leg movements. When writing, the last two strokes (left and right diagonal) should be changed to a vertical and an upward stroke. “土” (tǔ) radical, which is related to land, buildings, or natural environments. The last stroke should be written as an upward stroke instead of a horizontal line.



In the text, for the character “地” (de) – Students need to recognize its function as a particle used after an adverb (adverb + 地 + verb), such as “高兴地说” ("happily said"). This is different from the “得” (de) in Lesson 7, which appears after a verb (verb + 得 + adverb), such as “做得好” ("did well"). The word “觉得” (jué de) means "to think" or "to feel."



We also learned a new sentence Pattern: “月亮会不会跟人走？” This is a yes/no question that uses affirmative + negative (会不会) to form the question. Students should practice making their own sentences using this pattern and answer with “会” (yes) or “不会” (no). We will continue practicing this sentence structure next class.
