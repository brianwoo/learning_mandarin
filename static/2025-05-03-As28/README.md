Hi Parents and Students,



Good afternoon. Today we successfully finished Lesson 11. At the beginning of the class, we reviewed the characters, words, and text from the previous lesson. Then, students did a short dictation to check how well they remembered the new characters and words. The dictation has been marked and returned. Parents are welcome to take a look to see how your child is doing.

 

In today’s new content, we focused on practicing the sentence pattern:

"Subject + 从(from)... + verb + 到(to) + object."

Students worked in groups to create and share their own sentences, and they did a great job expressing themselves confidently.

 

We also learned how to use the adverb “最 (zuì)”, which means “most” and is used before adjectives or adverbs to show the highest degree—just like the superlative form in English. To help students better understand, we practiced using “最” through some fun Q&A activities in class.

 

After that, we read the text 《泉水和大海》(Spring Water and Sea) together. From this lesson, we learned four new characters: 泉 (spring), 海 (sea), 进 (enter), 溪 (stream). We compared these characters with the ones we learned last time and focused on how their structures and meanings relate to water. Students discovered that many characters with the “氵” radical are connected to water, and they showed great thinking during the comparison.

3. Complete the characters writing practice for Lesson 11.(see attachment). Please pay attention to stroke order and the correct structure of each character.

 

Next class, we will begin Lesson 12, and I encouraged my students to preview the texts of the new lesson.
