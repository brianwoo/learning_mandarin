Hi Parents and Students,

Good afternoon! We began today’s class with a quick review of characters from the previous unit. This helped students refresh their knowledge and smoothly transition into new content. After the review, we moved on to Lesson 10 , focusing on the lesson titled “Spring Rain.”

In this lesson, we focused on learning 12 new Chinese characters: 种 (zhǒng/ zhòng), 吧, 要, 发, 芽, 苗, 树, and 瓜. In class, we practiced not only the pronunciation and meanings of these characters, but also how to write them correctly with proper stroke order.

When reading the text aloud, students were reminded to pronounce each word accurately. We paid special attention to the character “种”, which has two pronunciations and meanings: When pronounced with the third tone, it is a noun meaning “seed." When pronounced with the fourth tone, it is a verb meaning "to plant." Please to be mindful of the tone and meaning when reviewing at home. After learning the characters, we also practiced word formation to help students understand and remember new vocabulary in context.

The key sentence structure in this lesson was: “我要种瓜。” (“I want to plant melons.”) We focused on the use of the word “要”, which is a modal verb expressing intention or desire to do something. In class, we practiced replacing “种瓜” with other verbs like “玩游戏” (play games) or “看书” (read books) to help students use the sentence pattern flexibly and express different ideas. In our next lesson, we will continue working with this sentence pattern to further strengthen students’ comprehension and speaking skills.

通过这篇课文，我们重点学习了12个新的汉字：种（三声和四声）、吧、要、发、芽、苗、树、瓜。课堂上，我们不仅学习了这些汉字的拼音读音、意思，还进行了正确书写的练习。练习课文朗读时，同学们需要注意发音准确。同时，我们特别提醒大家，“种”有两个读音，读三声时，是名字表示“种子”；读四声时，是一个动词，表示“种植”，孩子们复习时要区分清楚它们的发音和用法。在识字练习后，我们还进行了组词活动。以帮助孩子在语境中理解和记忆新字。

 

本课的重点句型是：“我要种瓜。” 我们通过这个句子重点学习了“要”的用法。在这句话中，“要”是一个助动词，表示做某事的意志。课堂上我们进行了句型替换练习，例如将“种瓜”替换为“玩游戏”、“看书”等，帮助孩子们灵活运用句型表达不同的意思。下节课我们将继续围绕这个句型进行更多练习，加强孩子们的理解和口语表达能力。
