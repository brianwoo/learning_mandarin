Good afternoon! In today’s class, we focused on practicing the "yes/no" question structure using “会不会”, such as “月亮会不会跟人走？” (Will the moon follow people?). Students created many interesting sentences by replacing different words, like “你会不会讲中文？” (Can you speak Chinese?) and “你们会不会去公园玩？” (Will you go to the park?).

We also practiced a dialogue from page 59 of the textbook in pairs. During the activity, students worked together to express their ideas in complete sentences, which helped improve their speaking skills.

When learning the nursery rhyme “弯弯的月亮 A Crescent Moon”, students practiced recognizing and forming words with new characters: 弯wān,船chuán,只zhǐ,往wǎng,球 qiú,tíng. One important note is that “只” is a polyphonic character with different meanings based on pronunciation: zhǐ (只) means “only,” such as “我只有一本书。” (I only have one book); zhī (只) is a measure word for animals, like “一只小鸟” (a little bird). Please pay attention to the pronunciation while reading.

All students listened carefully and actively participated in reading activities. To reinforce learning, I recommend reviewing the new characters, dialogues, and nursery rhyme at home.

在学习儿歌《弯弯的月亮》时，孩子们对 “弯、船、只、往、球、停” 这些生字进行了认读和组词练习。特别需要注意的是“只”是一个多音字，在不同的语境下读音和意思都会有所不同，当它读作zhǐ时，表示“仅仅”，例如“我只有一本书。”；当它读作zhī时，是量词，用来形容动物，例如“一只小鸟”。读课文时请注意区分。

课堂上，同学们认真聆听，积极朗读。建议回家后继续复习生字、课文对话和儿歌，多加练习，完成作业以巩固所学内容。
