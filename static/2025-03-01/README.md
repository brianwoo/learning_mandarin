The key sentence pattern for this lesson was “为什么河水会流动？” (Why does the river flow?). Through this structure, students learned how to ask questions using “为什么” (why). For speaking practice, we focused on two important structures: “为什么” (why) and “会不会” (can or not). Through dialogue exercises, students learned how to use these phrases in real conversations, enhancing their speaking skills.

Our reading material for today was a fun and catchy children’s rhyme 《小好奇》. We paid special attention to the pronunciation of “好”, which should be read as the fourth tone (hào) in this rhyme.
